Legend and non-negotiable rules
BOOSTPaid promotion. Always via Meta Ads Manager or LinkedIn Campaign Manager. Never via the in-app boost button (30% more expensive).
InstagramTalent-only audience. Every post ends with a CTA to the talent pool from M2 onward.
LinkedInFounder / company audience. Claudia and Laia post as individuals primarily. Company page for boosted content only.
SubstackBridge content. Every article serves both talent and founders. Dual CTA at the end of every issue.
CollabPaid micro-influencer collabs (€75–100/post) + free guest reshares. Every episode guest gets formatted clips within 48h of publish.
Email / SEOEmail capture live from W1. SEO pages built with Claude — 1 per month. Direct guest outreach from M3.
TikTokFree. Repost every Instagram Reel to TikTok without watermark. 10 mins/week. Not in budget — zero cost.
The 3 rules that override everything else
Never boost coldWait 48 hours of organic performance. Only boost posts with above-average saves and shares. If a post underperforms organically, no amount of budget will save it.
Consistency beats perfection5 posts/week every week beats 10 posts one week and 2 the next. The algorithm rewards consistency above everything else.
Email list before follower countEvery euro spent on Instagram should funnel toward email sign-ups, not just follows. A list of 3,000 is worth more than 20,000 followers when selling to companies.
